PRODUCTION FORMULA

One watchability rating

Drama + exceptional player production + game magnitude + matchup strength + realized Star Power, capped at 10.

Exceptional player production

Credit starts above 40 points, 15 assists, 20 rebounds or 8 steals + blocks. Full axis credit at 60 / 25 / 30 / 12 respectively. Efficiency and a triple-double support an already exceptional performance. Turnovers reduce credit.

Strongest player + 25% of the runner-up, up to 4 points. Published rank does not affect performance credit.

Other contributions

Drama: clutch time, lead changes, comeback and overtime. Game magnitude: extreme leads, quarters and scoring droughts, up to 3 points. Record strength: up to 2. Star Power: rank × actual minutes, up to 1.5.

Thresholds are explicit heuristics. Historical rarity, exact playoff stakes and tactical quality are still unavailable.
